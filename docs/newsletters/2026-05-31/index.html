Broad Brand Reach Needs Deeper Relevance for Specific Needs

The article highlights that major athletic apparel brands like Nike and Adidas have significant "reach" (familiarity) but a "permission gap" where their relevance for specific athletic activities is low. This exemplifies Mental Availability, showing that while broad awareness is achieved, brands must also cultivate strong associations with diverse category entry points to be chosen for particular occasions. For Mars Asia Snacking, this means ensuring our highly familiar brands are not just known, but specifically associated with various snacking moments and emotional needs.
