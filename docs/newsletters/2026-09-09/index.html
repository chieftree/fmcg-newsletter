Strong Distinctive Assets Crucial for Brand Recognition

The Campaign UK article highlights the enduring importance of distinctive brand assets (DBAs) in driving brand recognition and recall. It emphasizes that easily identifiable visual and auditory cues create mental shortcuts, allowing consumers to quickly recognize a brand amidst market clutter. For Mars snacking brands, a consistent audit and reinforcement of DBAs like the 스니커즈 (Snickers) logo, 엠앤엠즈 (M&M's) characters, or 트윅스 (Twix) colors across all touchpoints is essential to maintain strong recognition and differentiation.
